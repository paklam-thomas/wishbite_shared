Privacy policy

私隱政策

Your places are yours.

你嘅地方，只屬於你。

Last updated 10 November 2026

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 10 日

Somedate keeps everything on your phone. There's no Somedate account, and Somedate has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and sells nothing. Somedate's only server is the one Somedate AI uses, if you choose it — and it keeps no posts.
遲啲去所有嘢都save喺你部手機。遲啲去冇帳戶，冇廣告、冇分析、冇追蹤，亦唔會賣任何資料。遲啲去唯一嘅伺服器係你揀用遲啲去提供嘅 AI Plan先會用到，佢唔會留低任何 post。

What stays on your phone

留喺你手機嘅資料

The places you save, their photos, your notes and tags, and your reminder settings. They leave your phone only if you send or back up a list yourself (Settings › Lists).

你儲存嘅地方、相片、備註同標籤，同提醒設定。只有你自己 send 出去或者備份清單（設定 › 清單）先會離開你部手機。

Your location

你嘅位置

Used on your phone to show places near you and to remind you when you walk past one. The phone does the watching; your location is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

只喺你部手機用嚟顯示附近嘅地方，同埋行過嗰陣提醒你。由手機負責留意；你嘅位置唔會send俾開發者或者任何人。

AndroidIf you set Location to Allow all the time, Somedate uses your location for these reminders even when the app is closed or not in use.

Android如果你喺 Android用家，你將位置權限設做「一律允許」，遲啲去喺 App 關咗或者冇用緊嘅時候，都會用你嘅位置嚟提醒你。

Screenshots you share

你分享嘅截圖

The words in a screenshot are read on your phone. The screenshot stays on the phone unless you choose an online AI (below).

截圖入面嘅字喺你部手機度讀。除非你揀用網上 AI（見下文），否則截圖唔會離開你部手機。

iPhoneRead by Apple's Vision.

iPhone用 Apple 嘅 Vision 讀。

AndroidRead by Google's ML Kit, which sends Google basic diagnostics — the phone's model, Android and app versions, how long reading took, and an ID for this install — never the screenshot or its words. Google's privacy policy applies to them.

Android用 Google 嘅 ML Kit 讀。ML Kit 會 send 基本診斷資料俾 Google——手機型號、Android 同 App 版本、讀咗幾耐，同埋呢次安裝嘅一個 ID——但唔會 send 截圖或者入面嘅字。呢啲資料受 Google 私隱政策約束。

The map

地圖

iPhoneThe map is Apple Maps.

iPhoneiPhone用家嘅地圖係 Apple 地圖。

AndroidThe map is drawn from OpenFreeMap, with OpenStreetMap data. To show it, your phone fetches the map for the area on screen, so OpenFreeMap's servers see your IP address and which area you're looking at — not your location or your places.

AndroidAndroid用家嘅地圖由 OpenFreeMap 提供，用 OpenStreetMap 嘅資料。為咗顯示地圖，你部手機會需要攞畫面上嗰個範圍嘅地圖資料，所以 OpenFreeMap 嘅伺服器會見到你嘅 IP 地址同你睇緊邊個範圍——但唔會見到你嘅位置或者你嘅地方。

Links you share

你分享嘅連結

To find the place, Somedate opens the link's public preview (the picture and caption any app shows for a link) and searches for the shop with the phone's own map search: Apple Maps on iPhone, Google's on Android. When the post gives a Hong Kong street address, that address alone is also looked up in the Hong Kong Government's Address Lookup Service (als.gov.hk), to put the pin in the right building. Apple's or Google's privacy policy applies to those map searches. Address data: Address Lookup Service, the Government of the Hong Kong SAR, from DATA.GOV.HK.

為咗搵出地方，遲啲去會打開連結嘅公開預覽（任何 App 顯示連結時見到嘅相同文字），再用手機本身嘅地圖搜尋搵間舖：iPhone 用 Apple 地圖，Android 用 Google 嘅。如果 post 有香港街道地址，就只會將個地址拎去香港政府嘅地址查詢服務（als.gov.hk）查，將圖釘放喺啱嘅大廈。地圖搜尋受 Apple 或者 Google 嘅私隱政策約束。地址資料：香港特別行政區政府地址查詢服務，來自 DATA.GOV.HK。

The AI that reads posts

讀 post 嘅 AI

By default no AI is used: Somedate finds the shop from the post's own words (📍 lines, addresses, districts) on your phone, and nothing is sent to an AI. If you choose an online AI with your own key — GLM by Z.ai, Grok by xAI, OpenAI, Claude by Anthropic, Gemini by Google, or a service you set up yourself — and allow it, the post's text and the screenshots you share are sent to that service to find the shop and dish, straight from your phone and never through Somedate. Z.ai is part of Zhipu AI, a company based in Beijing, China (its international service is run from Singapore); xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic and Google are based in the USA. That service's privacy policy applies. You can switch this off at any time in Settings › AI.

預設唔會用 AI：遲啲去喺你部手機由 post 本身嘅內容（📍、地址、地區）嘗試搵出店舖，乜都唔會傳去 AI。如果你自己有AI既API key (Bring your own key)，例如 Z.ai 嘅 GLM、xAI 嘅 Grok、OpenAI、Anthropic 嘅 Claude、Google 嘅 Gemini，或者你自己嘅AI，並且知道同准許，post 嘅文字同你分享嘅截圖會由你部手機直接傳去嗰個AI 搵出店舖同食物，咁你就可以好方便。留意返，呢啲你send 俾AI嘅資料喺唔會經過遲啲去，因為我哋唔想知，同時想Keep唔用server。

重要： Z.ai 屬於總部喺中國北京嘅智譜 AI（國際服務由佢嘅新加坡公司營運；xAI、OpenAI、Anthropic 同 Google 嘅總部喺美國。受嗰個服務地區同公司嘅私隱政策約束。你隨時可以喺 設定 › AI 關閉。

Somedate AI

遲啲去 AI

If you choose Somedate AI (Settings › AI) and allow it, the post's text and the screenshots you share go through Somedate's server to xAI (Grok, USA) — or to Z.ai (GLM, China) if you pick the GLM plan — to find the shop and dish. The server passes them on and keeps no copy. Choosing No AI or your own key leaves the server out entirely.

如果你揀遲啲去 AI（設定 › AI）並且准許，post 嘅文字同你分享嘅截圖會經遲啲去嘅伺服器傳去 xAI（Grok，美國）——如果你揀 GLM 計劃就傳去 Z.ai（GLM，中國）——搵出店舖同食物。伺服器只會轉交，唔會留低副本。揀唔用 AI 或者用自己嘅 key，就完全唔會經伺服器。

iPhoneThe server keeps only a random ID for your install (checked with Apple's App Attest, so only the real app can use it), how many posts it has read for it, and the App Store plan you bought — no name, email, Apple ID or location. Apple's DeviceCheck notes on Apple's side whether your iPhone has had the free posts.

iPhone佢只會記住你部裝置嘅一個隨機 ID（用 Apple 嘅 App Attest 核對，所以只有正版 App 先用到）、幫佢讀咗幾多個 post，同你喺 App Store 買咗邊個計劃——冇名、冇電郵、冇 Apple ID、冇位置。Apple 嘅 DeviceCheck 會喺 Apple 嗰邊記低你部 iPhone 用過免費 post 未。

AndroidThe server keeps only a scrambled ID for your phone (checked with Google Play Integrity, so only the real app can use it; only Somedate gets this ID, so reinstalling doesn't start the free posts again, and it can't be turned back into the phone's own ID), how many posts it has read for it, and the plan you bought on Google Play — no name, email, Google account or location.

Android佢只會記住你部手機嘅一個打亂咗嘅 ID（用 Google Play Integrity 核對，所以只有正版 App 先用到；呢個 ID 只有遲啲去攞到，所以重裝都唔會重新開始免費 post，亦冇辦法變返做手機本身嘅 ID）、幫佢讀咗幾多個 post，同你喺 Google Play 買咗邊個計劃——冇名、冇電郵、冇 Google 帳戶、冇位置。

Crash reports

當機報告

iPhoneOff unless you turn them on (Settings › Crash reports). Then, if Somedate crashes, a short report goes to the developer: what went wrong, the app version and the iOS version — nothing about you or your places.

iPhone預設關閉，除非你開啟（設定 › 當機報告）。開咗之後，如果遲啲去當機，會send一份簡短報告俾開發者：出咗咩問題、App 版本同 iOS 版本——唔包括你或者你嘅地方。

AndroidSomedate sends no crash reports. Google Play may show the developer crash counts from people who chose to share usage data with Google.

Android遲啲去唔會 send 任何當機報告；如果有人揀咗同 Google 分享使用資料，Google Play 可能會俾開發者睇到當機次數。

Deleting your data

刪除你嘅資料

Delete a place, delete or empty a list (Settings › Lists), or delete the app: everything is erased from your phone.

刪除一個地方、刪除或者清空清單（設定 › 清單），或者刪除 App：所有嘢都會喺你部手機抹走。

Children

兒童

Somedate isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

遲啲去唔係為兒童而設，亦唔會刻意收集兒童嘅任何資料。
