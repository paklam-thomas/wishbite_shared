Privacy policy

私隱政策

Your places are yours.

你嘅地方，只屬於你。

Last updated 18 October 2026

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日

Somedate keeps everything on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud for lists you share. There's no Somedate account or server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold.
遲啲去所有嘢都save喺你部 iPhone；你分享嘅清單就save喺你自己嘅 iCloud。遲啲去冇帳戶或者伺服器，冇廣告、冇分析、冇追蹤，亦唔會賣任何資料。

What stays on your iPhone

留喺你 iPhone 嘅資料

The places you save, their photos, your notes and tags, and your reminder settings. They leave your iPhone only if you share, send or back up a list yourself (Settings › Lists).

你儲存嘅地方、相片、備註同標籤，同提醒設定。只有你自己分享、send 出去或者備份清單（設定 › 清單）先會離開你部 iPhone。

Shared lists and iCloud

分享清單同 iCloud

When you share a list, its places and photos are stored in the list owner's iCloud (Apple's CloudKit) and shown to the people invited to it, along with the names from their iCloud accounts and the stars each person gives. Lists you don't share never go to iCloud. The developer can't see any of it. Removing someone, stopping sharing or leaving takes the list off their iPhone (they get 7 days to keep a copy). Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

你分享一張清單嘅時候，入面嘅地方同相片會Save喺清單主人嘅 iCloud（Apple 嘅 CloudKit），被邀請嘅人先可以睇到，所有人iCloud 帳戶嘅名同俾每個地方嘅星都喺。冇用分享功能嘅清單唔會上iCloud。總之，開發者乜都睇唔到亦都唔想睇。如果移除某人、停止分享清單或者退出，張清單就會喺佢部 iPhone 消失（佢有 7 日時間儲存一份，唔係既話佢好慘）。iCloud 受 Apple 私隱政策約束。

Your location

你嘅位置

Used on your iPhone to show places near you and to remind you when you walk past one. iOS does the watching; your location is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

只喺你部 iPhone 用嚟顯示附近嘅地方，同埋行過嗰陣提醒你。由 iOS 負責留意；你嘅位置唔會send俾開發者或者任何人。

Links you share

你分享嘅連結

To find the place, Somedate opens the link's public preview (the picture and caption any app shows for a link) and searches Apple Maps for the shop. When the post gives a Hong Kong street address, that address alone is also looked up in the Hong Kong Government's Address Lookup Service (als.gov.hk), to put the pin in the right building. Apple's privacy policy applies to Maps searches. Address data: Address Lookup Service, the Government of the Hong Kong SAR, from DATA.GOV.HK.

為咗搵出地方，遲啲去會打開連結嘅公開預覽（任何 App 顯示連結時見到嘅相同文字），再喺 Apple 地圖搵間舖。如果 post 有香港街道地址，就只會將個地址拎去香港政府嘅地址查詢服務（als.gov.hk）查，將圖釘放喺啱嘅大廈。地圖搜尋受 Apple 私隱政策約束。地址資料：香港特別行政區政府地址查詢服務，來自 DATA.GOV.HK。

The AI that reads posts

讀 post 嘅 AI

By default no AI is used: Somedate finds the shop from the post's own words (📍 lines, addresses, districts) on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to an AI. If you choose an online AI with your own key — GLM by Z.ai, Grok by xAI, OpenAI, Claude by Anthropic, Gemini by Google, or a service you set up yourself — and allow it, the post's text and the screenshots you share are sent to that service to find the shop and dish, straight from your iPhone and never through Somedate. Z.ai is part of Zhipu AI, a company based in Beijing, China (its international service is run from Singapore); xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic and Google are based in the USA. That service's privacy policy applies. You can switch this off at any time in Settings › AI.

預設唔會用 AI：遲啲去喺你部 iPhone 由 post 本身嘅內容（📍、地址、地區）嘗試搵出店舖，乜都唔會傳去 AI。如果你自己有AI既API key (Bring your own key)，例如 Z.ai 嘅 GLM、xAI 嘅 Grok、OpenAI、Anthropic 嘅 Claude、Google 嘅 Gemini，或者你自己嘅AI，並且知道同准許，post 嘅文字同你分享嘅截圖會由你部 iPhone 直接傳去嗰個AI 搵出店舖同食物，咁你就可以好方便。留意返，呢啲你send 俾AI嘅資料喺唔會經過遲啲去，因為我哋唔想知，同時想Keep唔用server。

重要： Z.ai 屬於總部喺中國北京嘅智譜 AI（國際服務由佢嘅新加坡公司營運；xAI、OpenAI、Anthropic 同 Google 嘅總部喺美國。受嗰個服務地區同公司嘅私隱政策約束。你隨時可以喺 設定 › AI 關閉。

Crash reports

當機報告

Off unless you turn them on (Settings › Crash reports). Then, if Somedate crashes, a short report goes to the developer: what went wrong, the app version and the iOS version — nothing about you or your places.

預設關閉，除非你開啟（設定 › 當機報告）。開咗之後，如果遲啲去當機，會send一份簡短報告俾開發者：出咗咩問題、App 版本同 iOS 版本——唔包括你或者你嘅地方。

Deleting your data

刪除你嘅資料

Delete a place, delete or empty a list (Settings › Lists), or delete the app: everything is erased from your iPhone. For a list you share, stop sharing first to remove it from iCloud.

刪除一個地方、刪除或者清空清單（設定 › 清單），或者刪除 App：所有嘢都會喺你部 iPhone 抹走。分享緊嘅清單，要先停止分享，先會喺 iCloud 刪除。

Children

兒童

Somedate isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

遲啲去唔係為兒童而設，亦唔會刻意收集兒童嘅任何資料。
