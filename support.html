How to use Somedate

點樣用遲啲去

Never forget a food post or place you wanted to try.

想去嘅美食同地方，唔會再唔記得。

Save a post

儲存 post

In Instagram or Threads, tap Share › Somedate. The first time, tap More and turn Somedate on. Somedate finds the shop and dish in the background and tells you when it's saved.

喺 Instagram 或者 Threads 撳 分享 › 遲啲去。第一次要撳 更多 開啟遲啲去。佢會喺背景搵出店舖同食物，存好會通知你。

Import your Instagram Saved

匯入你嘅 Instagram 收藏

In Somedate, tap Settings › Import from Instagram. It shows you each step.

In short: in Instagram, tap ☰ › Accounts Center (Meta Account) › Your information and permissions › Export your information › Create export.

  • Pick Export to device
  • Tick only Saved
  • Format JSON
  • Date range All time

When Instagram says it's ready, tap Download, then choose the zip in Somedate. No need to unzip. A big import keeps going while your iPhone is charging.

喺遲啲去撳 設定 › 由 Instagram 匯入，遲啲去會一步一步教你。

簡單講：喺 Instagram 撳 ☰ › 帳號管理中心（Meta 帳戶）› 你的資訊和權限 › 匯出你的資訊 › 建立匯出檔案。

  • 揀 匯出到裝置
  • 只剔「我的收藏」
  • 格式 JSON
  • 日期範圍 不限時間

等 Instagram 通知你個 zip 檔整好，撳 下載，再喺遲啲去揀個 zip，唔使解壓。post 多嘅話，充電時會繼續匯入。

Save a screenshot

儲存截圖

Took a screenshot of a post or a chat (WhatsApp, Instagram DM…)? In Photos, tap Share › Somedate. It reads the words in the picture to find the place. With an online AI on (Settings › AI), it also keeps the chat message as your note.

影咗 post 或者對話（WhatsApp、Instagram DM…）嘅截圖？喺相簿撳 分享 › 遲啲去，佢會讀相入面嘅字搵出地方。開咗網上 AI（設定 › AI）嘅話，對話訊息仲會記低做備註。

Add a place yourself

自己加地方

Tap the pink + Add at the bottom: paste post links, or add a place yourself.

撳底部粉紅色嘅 + 加入：貼上 post 嘅 Link，或者自己加地方。

It didn't find the shop

搵唔到店舖

Open the place and tap Change name or pick the right spot on the map. A screenshot of the post usually works best.

打開個地方，撳 改名 或者喺地圖揀返啱嘅位置。分享 post 嘅截圖通常最準。

Eat and Go

食同去

Want to go has two sides: Eat for restaurants, cafés and bakeries, and Go for exhibitions, pop-ups, hikes and anything else. Places with dates show when they start and end, and a limited-time dish keeps its last day.

想去 分兩邊：食 係餐廳、cafe 同麵包舖；去 係展覽、Pop-up、行山同其他地方。有日期嘅地方會顯示幾時開始、幾時完，限時 Menu 會記住最後一日。

Reminders

提醒

Settings › Reminders. Somedate reminds you when you walk near a saved bakery, dessert place or café — at most once a week per place. With more than one list, each list has its own kinds, distance and times (a new list starts off). For “only at lunch / evening”, allow Location › Always.

設定 › 提醒。行近儲存咗嘅麵包舖、甜品舖或者 cafe 會提醒你，每個地方一星期最多一次。有幾個清單嘅話，每個清單有自己嘅種類、距離同時間（新清單一開始係閂咗嘅）。

Lists

清單

Tap Me ▾ on the list or the map to switch lists or add one — e.g. the places you keep for a friend. Settings › Lists: rename a list, send it (they choose which of their lists it goes into), import into it, or delete it (you can save a copy first). Your first list can't be deleted, only emptied. Back up everything keeps every list, for a new iPhone.

喺清單或者地圖撳 我 ▾ 轉清單或者新增清單——例如幫朋友記低嘅地方。設定 › 清單：改名、send出去（對方揀放入佢邊個清單）、匯入，或者刪除（可以先儲存一份）。第一個清單唔可以刪除，只可以清空。備份全部 會保留所有清單，搬去新 iPhone 用。
